Build me an e-commerce email template builder that lets store owners create professional marketing emails in minutes using a drag-and-drop editor with 20+ pre-built templates for abandoned carts, product launches, and order confirmations. Include e-commerce specific components like product cards, discount codes, and countdown timers that export to clean, responsive HTML compatible with all major email clients. Users should be able to customize colors/fonts to match their brand, preview on mobile/desktop, and export directly to Shopify Email, Klaviyo, or download as HTML. Monetize with a freemium model: 5 free exports/month, then $29-49/month for unlimited exports and premium templates. Focus solely on e-commerce use cases - no generic email features.
